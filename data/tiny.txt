CHAPTER I

The blood was warm, and the night was still.
"My dear friend," said the Count, "we begin again."
Mina opened the letter. The ship moved through the dark sea.

CHAPTER II

Jonathan wrote that the castle stood above the forest. The wind moved around the stones, and somewhere below, a door closed.
"There are things here that I do not understand," he wrote. "Yet I must continue."

CHAPTER III

Lucy dreamed of red clouds over the harbour. Mina sat beside her until dawn and listened to the gulls. The doctor arrived before breakfast.
"Tell me everything," he said. "Leave out nothing."

CHAPTER IV

The Count smiled. The lamps trembled, though there was no wind in the room.
"Time is a strange country," he said. "A man may enter it and never find the same road home."

CHAPTER V

The ship moved through the black water. No sailor spoke. In the hold, beneath canvas and rope, the earth waited in its boxes.
The blood was warm. The night was still. Mina opened the letter again.

CHAPTER VI

"My dear friend," said the Count, "every ending is a door that has forgotten its hinge."
Jonathan heard footsteps in the corridor. They stopped outside his room, then began again farther away.

CHAPTER VII

At sunrise the mountains looked almost kind. The castle did not. Its windows held the last darkness long after the valley had turned gold.
Mina wrote one final line: We begin again.
